Every discipline, in-house

Each department brings its own expertise, and all of them work to one programme and one standard.

  • Design

    Architects, interior designers and engineers with more than 35 years of collective experience, from 2D layouts to 3D animation.

  • Planning

    Led by Planning Manager Mr. Robin Francisco, the team builds the contractual programme in Primavera P6 and tracks it with earned value reports.

  • Procurement

    Quality-focused sourcing of loose fittings, FF&E, finishes and lighting from around the world.

  • Fit-out factory

    Our joinery team manufactures counters, panelling, furniture and bespoke pieces in Musaffah.

  • Projects

    Project managers and site teams who coordinate every trade from first fix to handover.

  • Health, safety and environment

    HSE Manager Mr. Dennis Mutasa leads safety planning, toolbox talks and site inspections.

  • Sales

    Industry professionals with many years of experience who build lasting partnerships with our clients.

  • Finance and administration

    The team that keeps every project and every department running smoothly.
