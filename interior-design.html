Interior design

Commercial, residential and institutional interior design by a collaborative in-house team.

Design that is built to be delivered

Our designers bring more than 35 years of collective experience to every brief. The team is infused with a collaborative spirit that produces competitive solutions for our clients, proving that the sum of the whole is greater than its parts.

We have completed commercial, residential and institutional projects, exhibition and furniture design, and landscaping, delivering striking results for owners and stakeholders alike.

Whether you need a 2D layout, a 3D perspective or a 3D animation, Hyssna is the right partner to bring your space to life.

Our process, from first visit to handover

One team plans and controls every stage, so you deal with a single point of responsibility.

  1. Briefing and site visit

    We meet you on site to understand the space, how it needs to work, the brand standards and the opening date.

  2. Design

    Layouts, 3D perspectives and animations that bring the brief to life, with MEP design coordinated from the start.

  3. Planning

    A contractual programme in Primavera P6, tracked with weekly and monthly earned value reports.

  4. Procurement

    FF&E, finishes and lighting sourced from around the world and checked for quality before they reach site.

  5. Manufacture

    Counters, panelling and furniture made in our own joinery factory in Musaffah, Abu Dhabi.

  6. Delivery and handover

    Project managers coordinate every trade on site, under a documented safety plan, through to snagging and handover.

Frequently asked questions

If you cannot find the answer to your question, email us at info@hyssnauae.com.

What design outputs can you provide?

Space planning and 2D layouts, concept design, 3D perspectives and 3D animations, material and finish selections, and detailed drawings for construction.

Can you design and build?

Yes. Our design team works alongside our fit-out and joinery departments, so the design you approve is the one we deliver.

Do you design furniture and exhibitions?

Yes. We have completed furniture, exhibition and landscape design as well as commercial, residential and institutional interiors.
