Interior fit-out

Complete turnkey fit-out for offices, bank branches, retail and hospitality across the UAE.

Fit-out solutions that perform

We deliver complete interiors, ready to open, with every trade coordinated by one team. From strip-out and partitions to ceilings, floors, finishes and the associated mechanical, electrical and plumbing works, our project teams manage the whole fit-out.

Our planning engineers sequence every area in Primavera P6, and our own factory supplies the joinery, so programmes and quality stay under our control.

Bank branches are a particular strength. We have fitted out more than 25 branches for Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank alone, along with work for Emirates NBD, Emirates Islamic, NBAD and Al Hilal Bank.

What we deliver

  • Office and corporate fit-out

    Workplaces, boardrooms and collaboration floors.

  • Bank branches and ATM lobbies

    Customer halls, teller counters and private banking.

  • Partitions and false ceilings

    Glazed and solid partitions, doors and feature ceilings.

  • Lighting and feature walls

    Coordinated lighting, media walls and illuminated brand walls.

  • Retail and showrooms

    Stores, kiosks and shopping centre interiors.

  • Hospitality and lounges

    Cafés, lounges and guest spaces.

  • Associated MEP works

    Mechanical, electrical and plumbing, designed and installed.

  • Finishes

    Floors, wall finishes, wallpaper and decorative details.

Our process, from first visit to handover

One team plans and controls every stage, so you deal with a single point of responsibility.

  1. Briefing and site visit

    We meet you on site to understand the space, how it needs to work, the brand standards and the opening date.

  2. Design

    Layouts, 3D perspectives and animations that bring the brief to life, with MEP design coordinated from the start.

  3. Planning

    A contractual programme in Primavera P6, tracked with weekly and monthly earned value reports.

  4. Procurement

    FF&E, finishes and lighting sourced from around the world and checked for quality before they reach site.

  5. Manufacture

    Counters, panelling and furniture made in our own joinery factory in Musaffah, Abu Dhabi.

  6. Delivery and handover

    Project managers coordinate every trade on site, under a documented safety plan, through to snagging and handover.
