A heritage built on four decades of craft and integrity.

We all work together to create beautiful, functional spaces for our clients.

Crafting interiors across the UAE since 1980.

For more than four decades, Hyssna International has designed, planned and delivered commercial and domestic interiors across the United Arab Emirates.

Our team brings together architects, interior designers, graphic designers, fit-out specialists, civil, electrical and mechanical engineers, technicians and administration staff.

Passionate people

Our strength lies in teamwork: sharing strategies, knowledge and ideas across every department, with an open flow of communication and real team spirit.

In-house manufacturing

Our Musaffah joinery factory gives us control over quality, finish and lead times on every counter, panel and piece of furniture.

Respect and diversity

We respect the culture and values of the UAE, and that is reflected in our company and the multinational workforce we employ.

A legacy of excellence and integrity

From a single bank branch to programmes rolled out across the Emirates, we offer an A to Z solution: design, planning, procurement, our own joinery factory, site delivery and HSE, all under one roof.

Banks, government entities and global companies return to us for project after project, because we take responsibility for every stage.

Safety is planned in from day one

Our occupational health and safety management system covers every employee and subcontractor, whether office based or on site. It is led by our HSE Manager, Mr. Dennis Mutasa.

Each project has a documented safety plan. Subcontractors submit method statements and risk assessments for approval before work starts, and regular safety meetings, safety checks and toolbox talks keep standards high.

Early planning, effective prevention and control, personal protective equipment, accident reporting and good record keeping are all part of our safety policy.
