About us

Passionate people

Architects, designers, engineers, planners and craftsmen who share strategies, knowledge and ideas across every department.

Our own factory

Counters, panelling and furniture are made in our Musaffah joinery factory, so quality, finish and lead times stay in our hands.

One point of responsibility

Design, planning, procurement, manufacture, delivery and HSE under one roof, from the first layout to the final snag.

Safety and quality, planned in from day one.

Every project has a documented safety plan. Subcontractors submit method statements and risk assessments for approval before work starts, and regular safety meetings, site checks and toolbox talks keep standards high.

Our planning engineers build each programme in Primavera P6 and report progress weekly and monthly, so you always know where your project stands.

It is why the UAE's most demanding clients trust us with their branches, headquarters and public spaces.
