A leading interior contracting and design company in the UAE

We design, plan and build domestic and commercial interiors, from a single showroom to a branch network rolled out across the country.

Hyssna International offers an A to Z solution for every style of fit-out. Design, procurement, project management, HSE and our own fit-out factory work as one team, so each project has a single point of responsibility from the first drawing to handover.

Our portfolio covers commercial and residential buildings, villa communities, hospitals, shopping malls, high-rise towers, infrastructure and industrial projects.

What we deliver

Total solutions for turnkey projects under one roof, backed by in-house manufacturing.

  • Turnkey fit-out

    Complete interior works for offices, banks, retail and hospitality, delivered ready to open.

  • Interior design

    Space planning, concept design and detailed drawings prepared by our design department.

  • Joinery and custom furniture

    Counters, wall panelling, wardrobes and bespoke pieces made to drawing in our own factory.

  • Office furniture

    Workstations, meeting and executive furniture supplied and installed to suit the layout.

  • Carpets and flooring

    Carpet, vinyl and specialist flooring solutions for commercial and residential spaces.

  • Curtains and soft furnishings

    Curtains, blinds and finishing textiles that complete the interior.

One roof, every stage

Each stage is handled by our own department, so nothing is lost between design, the factory and site.

  1. Design

    Brief, space planning, concept and detailed drawings.

  2. Procurement

    Materials and finishes sourced and approved against the specification.

  3. Manufacture

    Joinery and furniture produced in our fit-out factory.

  4. Site delivery

    Our project team and HSE department run the works on site.

  5. Handover

    Snagging, final inspection and a space ready to use.
