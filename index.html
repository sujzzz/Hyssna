About Hyssna

How we deliver

One team, one roof, one point of responsibility

Interior finishing is a matter of sequence. Our own departments plan and control every stage, so your project is delivered on time and within budget.

  1. Design

    Architects, interior designers and engineers with more than 35 years of collective experience produce layouts, 3D perspectives and animations that bring the brief to life.

  2. Planning

    Our planning engineers build the contractual programme in Primavera P6, sequencing every area and tracking progress with weekly and monthly earned value reports.

  3. Procurement

    Loose fittings, FF&E, floor, wall and ceiling finishes and lighting are sourced globally, with budget review, quality control and supplier performance managed throughout.

  4. Joinery factory

    Counters, panelling, furniture and bespoke pieces are manufactured in our own Musaffah factory, so quality and lead times stay in our hands.

  5. Project delivery

    Experienced project managers and site teams coordinate every trade on site, from first fix to final finishes and snagging.

  6. Health, safety and environment

    A certified management system, project safety plans, toolbox talks and approved method statements keep our people, subcontractors and your site safe.

Safety is planned in from day one

Our occupational health and safety management system covers every employee and subcontractor, whether office based or on site. Each project has a documented safety plan, method statements and risk assessments are approved before work starts, and regular safety meetings and toolbox talks keep standards high.

Subcontractors are bound by the same policy, so the whole site works to one standard.
