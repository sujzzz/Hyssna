Joinery manufacturing

Custom joinery and furniture made in our own factory in Musaffah, Abu Dhabi.

From conceptualisation to execution

Hyssna Joinery handles everything from conceptualisation to execution. Working closely with architects and interior designers, we manufacture custom joinery and furniture for offices and homes across the UAE.

We work in engineered wood, laminated MDF, vinyl and veneer, and every piece is checked in the factory before it reaches site.

Combining our office interiors expertise with our own joinery production is what has made Hyssna a top-quality fit-out company across the UAE.

  • Reception counters

  • Wall panelling

Our process, from first visit to handover

One team plans and controls every stage, so you deal with a single point of responsibility.

  1. Briefing and site visit

    We meet you on site to understand the space, how it needs to work, the brand standards and the opening date.

  2. Design

    Layouts, 3D perspectives and animations that bring the brief to life, with MEP design coordinated from the start.

  3. Planning

    A contractual programme in Primavera P6, tracked with weekly and monthly earned value reports.

  4. Procurement

    FF&E, finishes and lighting sourced from around the world and checked for quality before they reach site.

  5. Manufacture

    Counters, panelling and furniture made in our own joinery factory in Musaffah, Abu Dhabi.

  6. Delivery and handover

    Project managers coordinate every trade on site, under a documented safety plan, through to snagging and handover.

Frequently asked questions

If you cannot find the answer to your question, email us at info@hyssnauae.com.

Where is your joinery made?

In our own factory in Musaffah, Abu Dhabi, where every piece is checked before it reaches site.

Which materials do you work with?

Engineered wood, laminated MDF, vinyl and veneer, finished to the architect's or designer's drawings.

Do you supply joinery to other contractors?

Yes. We work closely with architects, interior designers and contractors on offices and homes across the UAE.

Do you install what you make?

Yes. Our own teams install the joinery and coordinate with the other trades on site.
